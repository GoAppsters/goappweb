Samudrik — record-keeping for palmistry practitioners (Android and iOS)

My second app as designer, builder, and publisher, on Google Play and the App Store. Practitioners capture palm images in seconds, keep a profile per person, attach life events to look back on later, and discuss plates with peers. Uploaded images are tagged automatically with AI. Pave — the backend behind a cab-booking business

Django and PostgreSQL on AWS, with automated tests and deployment. Built from scratch in 2025 and extended ever since.
